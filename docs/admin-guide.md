## What Is the Admin Panel?

The **admin panel** is a special private section of the website that only you (or trusted people you give access to) can enter. From here, you can:

- See a summary of all customer inquiries and appointments
- Update car listings and prices
- Change the text and images on your homepage
- Track the status of each customer who has contacted you

Customers browsing the website cannot see or access any of this. It is for your eyes only.

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, or any browser)
2. Go to your website address and add `/admin/login` at the end

## The Admin Dashboard

After logging in, you will see your **Dashboard**. Think of it as your control centre.

At the top, you will see **summary numbers**:

| What You See | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Total Inquiries | How many customers have sent you a message through the website |
| New Inquiries | Customers you have not replied to yet |
| Total Appointments | How many people have requested a showroom visit or test drive |
| Pending Appointments | Appointments that need your confirmation |

Below the numbers, you will also see:

- **Recent Inquiries** — the latest messages from customers
- **Recent Appointments** — the latest appointment requests

On the left side (or top menu), you will see links to go to other sections of the admin panel.

## Managing Car Listings

Go to **Admin > Manage Cars** from the menu.

Here you can see all the Honda models listed on your website.

### To Edit a Car

1. Find the car model you want to update
2. Click the **Edit** button next to it
3. You can change:
   - **Name** — the model name
   - **Price** — the starting estimated price
   - **Short description** — a brief line about the car
   - **Best for** — who this car suits (e.g. families, young drivers)
   - **Visibility** — whether the car shows on the public website or not

4. Click **Save** when done

### To Show or Hide a Car

If a model is no longer available or you do not want to show it for now, you can **hide** it without deleting it:

1. Find the car in the list
2. Toggle the **Active / Inactive** switch
3. When inactive, the car will not appear to customers but your data is still saved

> **Tip:** It is better to hide a car than to delete it, so your records stay complete.

## Updating Your Homepage

Go to **Admin > Homepage Content** from the menu.

Your homepage has sections that you can change yourself without asking for help:

| Section | What You Can Change |
|---|---|
| **Hero Badge** | The small label at the top of the main banner (e.g. "Trusted Honda Sales Advisor") |
| **Hero Title** | The big headline text that customers see first |
| **Hero Subtitle** | The paragraph below the headline |
| **Hero Image** | The main photo on the banner |
| **Advisor Title** | The heading in the "About the Advisor" section |
| **Advisor Text** | The description paragraph about you and the showroom |
| **Advisor Image** | Your profile photo shown in that section |
| **Announcement Text** | A highlighted message for customers (e.g. current promotions or news) |
| **Call-to-Action Buttons** | The button labels and where they link to |

After making changes, click **Save** and your homepage will be updated immediately.

> **Important:** Changes are live as soon as you save. Double-check your spelling before saving.

## Viewing Customer Inquiries

Go to **Admin > Inquiries** from the menu.

Every time a customer fills in the inquiry form on your website, their message appears here.

### What You Will See

Each inquiry shows:

- Customer name and contact details (phone, email)
- Which Honda model they are interested in
- Their budget range
- Their message to you
- When they submitted it
- The **status** of the inquiry

### Inquiry Status

You can update the status of each inquiry to track your conversations:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Just received, not yet attended to |
| **Contacted** | You have already called or messaged this customer |
| **Won** | Customer has bought a car — congratulations! |
| **Lost** | Customer did not proceed with a purchase |

To update the status:
1. Click on the inquiry
2. Find the **Status** dropdown
3. Select the new status
4. Click **Save**

> **Tip:** Updating statuses regularly helps you keep track of which customers still need a follow-up call.

## Managing Appointments

Go to **Admin > Appointments** from the menu.

This section shows all appointment requests from customers who want to visit the showroom or arrange a test drive.

### What You Will See

Each appointment shows:

- Customer name and contact details
- Type of appointment (showroom visit, test drive, loan consultation)
- Preferred date and time
- Any special notes from the customer
- The **status** of the appointment

### Appointment Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Request received, waiting for your confirmation |
| **Confirmed** | You have confirmed the date and time with the customer |
| **Completed** | The appointment has taken place |
| **Cancelled** | The appointment was cancelled |

To update an appointment:
1. Find the appointment in the list
2. Click on it to open the details
3. Update the **Status** and add any notes if needed
4. Click **Save**

> **Tip:** When you confirm an appointment, remember to contact the customer personally to let them know the time is confirmed.

## Your Account Settings

From the **Admin Dashboard**, scroll down to find your **Account Settings** section.

Here you can:

- **Update your name, email, or phone number**
- **Change your password** — if you think someone else knows your password, change it immediately

### To Change Your Password

1. Scroll down on the Dashboard page to the **Change Password** section
2. Enter your **current password**
3. Enter your **new password** (must be at least 8 characters)
4. Enter the new password again to confirm
5. Click **Change Password**

> **Security tip:** Use a password that is hard to guess. Avoid using your name or simple words like "honda123".
